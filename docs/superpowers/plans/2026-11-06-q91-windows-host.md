### S5c: Exact-address lockout, /64 only slows guesses (security review of 53879c6)
- **Status:** Complete (uncommitted)
- Goal: S5b keyed the hard lockout and the pre-auth slot cap by /64, so one device on a shared /64 (home LAN, a Tailscale tailnet) can lock out every other device in it. Keep rotation useless without that.
- Acceptance: hard 10-failure lockout and the 4-per-sender pre-auth cap keyed by exact address (IPv4-mapped still folded to IPv4); a separate /64 failure count only adds delay (escalating, capped) and never refuses a correct PIN; tests: two addresses in one /64 do NOT share a lockout and each can still pair with the right PIN; rotating addresses in one /64 gets the growing delay; the 5th pre-auth socket from one exact address is refused while a neighbour in the same /64 connects; spec 3.1 updated; gate green; mutation results on changed files.
- Notes: `auth_throttle::Sender::of(IpAddr)` replaces `AuthThrottle::sender_key`: `address` (canonical IP, mapped IPv4 folded) keys the lockout and `try_pre_auth_slot`; `network` (IPv6 /64, IPv4 = the address) keys a second table that only adds delay. `attempt(&Sender, correct)`: a right PIN from a non-locked address is `Accepted` at once (no delay, no network check); a wrong PIN waits `max(own delay, network_delay_ms(n))`, where `network_delay_ms` is 0 up to 10 network failures (single device keeps the Mac's 200..1800, 0 table) then 200 ms x n capped at `MAX_NETWORK_DELAY_MS` 30 s (n=150); the lockout-starting failure also waits the network delay. The delay is slept before the `Incorrect PIN` reply while the socket holds its pre-auth slot (so throughput is at most 16 guesses per delay). A success does not reset the network count; it is GC'd after 1 h idle. `Host::handle_auth(id, &Sender, pin)`; logs use the exact address. Known trade (accepted by the review): 4 rotated addresses can still fill the global 16 pre-auth slots, as any 4 LAN hosts could.

### S5d: Per-network pre-auth cap, free slots on close, threat math (security review of 4e51360)
- **Status:** Complete (uncommitted)
- Goal: end the /64 back-and-forth with a written decision. The review asked for a hard /64 lockout; that is rejected (it would let one device lock out every neighbour, the finding fixed in S5c). Accepted instead: limit what one network can hold, and document why guessing stays impractical.
- Acceptance: at most 8 pre-auth sockets per network key (/64 for IPv6, the address for IPv4) inside the global 16, with the per-address 4 inside it (test: one /64 can hold 8, a 9th from a new address in it is refused while an IPv4 sender still connects); a pre-auth slot is released as soon as the peer closes, including during the wrong-PIN delay (test); spec 3.1 states the decision (no hard /64 lockout, why) and the threat math (8-digit PIN, at most 8 concurrent guesses per network at up to 30 s each => years on average); gate green; mutation results on changed files.
- Notes: `MAX_UNAUTHENTICATED_PER_NETWORK = 8` (`ConnectionLimits::max_unauthenticated_per_network`); `try_pre_auth_slot` takes the whole `Sender` and refuses `Full` / `SenderFull` / `NetworkFull` in that order; refusal log `8 sockets from network <net> already waiting for auth`. Close during the delay: the reader peeks the stream (`Peekable`) while `handle_auth` runs (`select!`, `biased` so the attempt is always recorded first); a close abandons the reply, any other frame stays queued and is handled after. Finding while doing it: freeing the slot on close alone would break the threat math (a right PIN is answered at once, a wrong one after its delay, so a peer could close on "no answer yet" and guess again at once). So each wrong PIN also holds a guess token (`host::GuessToken`) in a spawned task until its delay ends, socket or not; at 8 per network or 16 in all in their delay, the next `auth` is refused unchecked, right PIN or not, `Too many attempts; try again in 30s`, log `auth: <addr> refused, PIN not checked`. Spec 3.1: caps, release on close, guess budget, rejected hard /64 lockout and why, threat math (about 16 guesses/min per network, about 6 years on average; 32/min, about 3 years across many networks).
- Validation: `rtk proxy cargo test` 409 unit + 11 vectors + 65 integration passed (new: `one_slash_64_holds_at_most_eight_pre_auth_sockets_and_ipv4_still_connects`, `a_full_network_refuses_real_sockets_with_a_log_line_and_ipv4_still_connects`, `closing_during_the_wrong_pin_delay_frees_the_slot_at_once` (fails without the fix), `a_network_with_eight_wrong_pins_in_their_delay_gets_no_ninth_check`, `wrong_pins_in_their_delay_are_capped_across_networks_too`, `a_frame_sent_during_the_wrong_pin_delay_is_kept`); `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `--target x86_64-pc-windows-gnu` clean; `cargo fmt --check` ok; `printf 'QuipWindows/src/lib.rs\n' | tools/check.sh --files -` all green; `cargo mutants --file src/host.rs --file src/services/auth_throttle.rs --file src/services/ws_server.rs -j 4` (fixture env vars set): 221 tested, 165 caught, 10 missed, 41 unviable, 5 timeouts (count as caught); every miss is pre-existing code this slice did not change (`gc` lockout `>` x3, `ws_config` max_message_size, `is_benign_close` x5 and its `!`).

### S5e: A right PIN is never refused for other senders' guesses (security review of a52c5e2)
- **Status:** Complete (uncommitted)

- Notes: `handle_auth(id, &Sender, pin, slot: Option<PreAuthSlot>)` checks the PIN first (`throttle.attempt`); the pre-check refusal and its log are gone. Guess tokens are per network only (`GuessBudget`, `max_unauthenticated_per_network`); the all-senders cap and `all_guesses_in_delay` are removed. A wrong PIN takes a token at once or queues (`GuessBudget::take`, `tokio::sync::Notify` woken on every token drop) inside the spawned delay task; the queued PIN holds a clone of its socket's `PreAuthSlot` (now `Clone`, freed when the last clone drops) until it has a token, so a close while queued does not free the slot and the per-address 4 / per-network 8 socket caps bound the queue. The delay starts once the token is held; the reply is `Incorrect PIN` after it. Log `auth: <addr> wrong PIN queued: network <net> has 8 wrong PINs in their delay`; `Host::guesses_queued(network)` for tests. Authenticated re-auth passes `None` (queues without a slot). Spec 3.1: PIN checked first, queueing, no cross-network cap and why; threat math now per network (16/min per /64, ~40/h per IPv4 address via its lockout) and adds up across networks with no shared cap: a peer taking a whole IPv4 /24 gets ~10^4/h, ~7 months on average (S5d's 16-in-all cap had bounded that at ~3 years).
